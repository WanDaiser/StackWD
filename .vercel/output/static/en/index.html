Four layers, one clear process.

What happens at each stage, and what you receive at the end of it, is clear from the start. You work directly with the same person throughout.

  1. Strategy

    Discovery

    We start by listening to your business, your audience and your competitors, then agree on what the site must achieve.

    From you
    Your goals, plus any existing content and brand files.
    Delivered
    Scope, sitemap and timeline.

  2. Interface

    Design

    Content structure and interface come first as wireframes, then as detailed design. Desktop and mobile are designed together.

    From you
    Feedback on the drafts.
    Delivered
    Approved interface design and a clickable prototype.

  3. Code

    Development

    The design becomes fast, accessible code that is easy to update. You follow progress on a live preview link.

    From you
    Final content and notes on the preview.
    Delivered
    A tested site, ready to launch.

  4. Launch

    Launch

    We set up the domain, hosting, analytics and search settings, then put the site live. Support continues after launch.

    From you
    Domain access and final approval.
    Delivered
    Live site, a short guide and care options.

Frequently asked questions

If your question is not here, just write to us.

How long does a website project take?

It depends on the scope. A single landing page and a multilingual corporate site do not follow the same timeline. After the first call we share a written, stage-by-stage schedule.

How is pricing done?

Each project is priced by scope. After the discovery call we send a written proposal that lists exactly what is included. Anything outside that scope is discussed in advance.

Who prepares the content?

You can provide the text and images. We guide you on content structure and page headings. If copywriting is needed, it appears as a separate line in the proposal.

Can I update the site myself after launch?

Yes. When needed, an easy-to-use content management panel is set up and a short guide is included.

Who owns the domain and hosting?

You do. Accounts are opened under your control and we set them up together.

Will a redesign affect our search rankings?

With a well-planned migration, rankings are preserved. Old addresses are redirected to new pages, metadata is carried over and results are monitored after the switch.

Is working with a one-person studio a risk?

A fair question. That is why the source code, accounts and documents stay in your hands from day one. The process is documented, so another developer could pick it up if ever needed.
